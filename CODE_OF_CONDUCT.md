# Code of conduct

Keep discussions constructive and focused on the work. Disagreement about designs is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination and sharing someone else's private information are not.

Respect contributors' time and differing levels of experience. Give credit where it is due, report problems with evidence, and avoid demanding unpaid support.

Repository maintainers may moderate discussions, remove abusive content or restrict participation to protect contributors. For a sensitive incident, request a private contact channel from a maintainer rather than posting private details publicly.
